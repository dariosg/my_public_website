I am a Professor at the [Universidad de Zaragoza](https://www.unizar.es/),
Spain, affiliated with the [Computer Architecture Group](https://gaz.i3a.es/)
within the [Computer Science and Engineering
Department](https://diis.unizar.es/) and the [Aragon Institute for Engineering
Research (I3A)](https://www.i3a.es). My work gravitates around computer systems
from hardware implementation to runtime systems, including heterogeneous
systems, energy efficiency, memory hierarchy, AI for systems, ...

Prior to my academic career, I spent time as a researcher at Qualcomm Research Silicon Valley,
working on heterogeneous programming models, an experience that continues to shape my research
agenda. I graduated from the Universidad de Zaragoza in 2011.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like a copy of my CV or check the official
university version [here](https://janovas.unizar.es/sideral/CV/dario-suarez-gracia).
