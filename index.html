I am a Professor at the Universidad de Zaragoza, Spain, affiliated with the Computer Architecture Group within the Computer Science and Engineering Department and the Aragon Institute for Engineering Research (I3A). My work gravitates around computer systems from hardware implementation to runtime systems, including heterogeneous systems, energy efficiency, memory hierarchy, AI for systems, …

Prior to my academic career, I spent time as a researcher at Qualcomm Research Silicon Valley, working on heterogeneous programming models, an experience that continues to shape my research agenda. I graduated from the Universidad de Zaragoza in 2011.
